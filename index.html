Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is consultation really free?

Yes. Every customer can get free consultation, every time they contact us.

Is the first hour free for every customer?

Yes. The first 60 minutes of technical support are free for every new support session, including returning customers.

What is the charge after the free hour?

₹25 per additional hour. We will explain the charge and get your approval before continuing paid work.

What is included in ₹199 membership?

Membership gives priority booking, free consultation, member support assistance and ticket request tracking. Technical work is charged separately at the agreed rate. Membership lasts 30 days from confirmation.

Does membership renew automatically?

No. Membership is manually confirmed and does not automatically renew.

How do I book?

Use the support ticket form or contact us through WhatsApp. Your request is confirmed after we acknowledge it.

Can you fix my computer remotely?

Remote support may be possible for compatible problems. It requires your permission and a suitable remote support application. Never share your password or OTP.

Do you offer physical repair or home visits?

This website is for phone-based and remote guidance. Ask us separately about availability of physical repair or on-site visits.
