Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before you contact us.

Is the first consultation free?

Eligible first-time customers receive up to 60 minutes of free initial consultation.

What happens after 60 minutes?

A ₹49 continuation support charge applies after the initial free consultation. This is not the full repair cost.

Do you provide onsite support?

Onsite support may be available depending on your location and scheduling. Additional visit or travel charges may apply and will be discussed first.

Can you solve problems remotely?

Remote guidance may be available. Any remote access to your device will only happen with your permission.

Are spare parts included?

No. Hardware parts, software licences and other additional services are charged separately where applicable, with your approval.

How can I submit a review?

Use the Share Your Experience form above. It opens WhatsApp with your feedback ready to send. Reviews are checked before publication.
